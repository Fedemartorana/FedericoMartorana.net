Archive Exhibit Inhabit

Year
January 2025
Focus
Archive and public space
Type
Academic / Cultural project

An extension of CASVA that refuses to become an isolated object: archive, exhibition, work and housing are folded into the existing rhythms of QT8 and the landscape of Monte Stella. Concept Archive, exhibition spaces, ateliers and housing form one continuous intervention beside the former QT8 market. The project borrows the district's structural rhythms, pilotis and indirect routes instead of placing another autonomous object in the landscape. System An exposed-concrete platform connects CASVA to Monte Stella. Museum spaces sit below it; patios cut through its mass; a portico, archive and linear residential block complete the sequence. Solids, voids and thresholds make culture, work and inhabitation part of the same public ground. Credits PROJECT TEAM: ALESSANDRA CASTELLI, GIULIA CAGLIO, FEDERICO MARTORANA. PROFESSORS: Stamatina Kousidi, Mario Scaglia. TUTORS: Matteo Lorenzo, Martina Simonati.
