ERMA TENE A Place for the Monteverdi Festival

Year
2026
Location
Cremona, Italy
Type
Academic / Conservation

The church is not treated as an empty shell waiting for a fit-out. A second, reversible ground and a family of autonomous columns introduce everything the festival needs without pretending to be old. Concept ERMA TENE adapts the Church of SS. Marcellino e Pietro for the Monteverdi Festival through a small number of dry, reversible interventions. Survey, acoustic study and diagnosis establish the limits of the project; traces of time stay visible. System A floating technical floor protects the historic pavement and changes configuration for concerts, exhibitions and rehearsals. Slender opaline-glass columns contain light, air, sound and safety equipment, keeping every new service independent from the church fabric. Credits Project team: Cristian Casiraghi, Federico Martorana, Anita Rizzi, Arturo delli Ponti, Chiara Tabladini. Professors: Angelo Giuseppe Landi, Annalisa Galante.
