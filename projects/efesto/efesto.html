EFESTO

Year
2026
Location
Crema, Italy
Type
Academic / Adaptive reuse

The former Stramezzi Ironworks is kept rough, legible and productive. A raised route crosses the railway fracture and turns the complex into a civic machine for work, culture and housing. Concept EFESTO reuses the former Stramezzi Ironworks without cleaning away its industrial identity. The existing bays, sawtooth roofs and steel frame become the base for a mixed civic programme rather than a single-function redevelopment. System An autonomous steel walkway crosses the railway fracture and carries movement, exhibitions and access. Below it, reconfigurable workshops occupy the old hall; along its quieter stretch, the route becomes a residential gallery serving duplex and studio units. Survey and diagnosis define what can be repaired, retained or touched. credits Project team: Cristian Casiraghi, Federico Martorana, Anita Rizzi, Chiara Tabladini. Professors: Christian Campanella, Roberto Felicetti, Guidarini Stefano, Franco Guzzetti.Politecnico di Milano, Architectural Design Studio, A.A. 2025/2026.
