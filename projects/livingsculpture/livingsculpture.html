living sculpture

when jan. 2024 why The question I then asked myself was: why can’t our homes be sculptures? Or why can’t sculptures become our homes? Sculpture, in its simplified and conceptual form, is already used to experiment with and test the perceptions and characteristics of environments and spaces that are in the design phase; there is already a sort of contamination between the two art forms. The demonstration of this thesis is pragmatic: it is based on the creation of a fully functional and qualitatively acceptable dwelling, complete with all services, within a Chillida sculpture. I chose the sculpture Casa de Luz III.
