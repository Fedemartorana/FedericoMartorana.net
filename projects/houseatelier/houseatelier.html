House Atelier

Year
June 2024
Context
Lakeside landscape
Type
Academic / House and studio

A house and a studio share the same section but refuse to behave the same way: the lower volume is blunt and structural, the upper one hides its weight behind a perforated skin. Concept A house for an artist overlooking the lake. Two stacked volumes separate domestic life from work: an exposed structural base carries a studio wrapped in micro-perforated metal, making the heavier body appear mute and weightless. Credits Federico Martorana.
